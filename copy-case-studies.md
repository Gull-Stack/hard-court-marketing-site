# Hard Court Marketing - Case Studies

## Case Study 1: Precision Pro Courts
**The Challenge of Premium Positioning**

### Background
Precision Pro Courts, a tennis and pickleball installation company in Northern California, was struggling to differentiate themselves in a crowded market. Despite 15 years of experience and ASBA certification, they were:

- Competing primarily on price with unlicensed contractors
- Averaging $12K per project (mostly residential)
- Getting 80% price-shopping leads from generic contractor directories
- Invisible in Google searches for commercial court projects
- Missing out on $50K+ municipal and school district contracts

### Our Strategy
**Phase 1: Authority Website Development**
- Complete website redesign with ASBA certification prominence
- Professional portfolio showcasing $100K+ commercial projects
- Technical content demonstrating expertise (surface specifications, drainage systems, etc.)
- Case studies of complex installations (slope challenges, multi-sport conversions)

**Phase 2: Commercial SEO Campaign**
- Targeted "tennis court contractor [city]" for 15 surrounding municipalities
- "Pickleball court installation commercial" keyword strategy
- Educational content: "Tennis Court Resurfacing Guide," "Pickleball Court Dimensions Guide"
- Local citation building with focus on commercial directories (BBB, commercial contractor associations)

**Phase 3: Lead Qualification System**
- AI chatbot screening for budget, timeline, and project type
- Automatic routing: residential leads to competitor referral program, commercial leads prioritized
- Lead scoring based on property type (municipal, school, club, commercial)

### Results After 120 Days
**Website Traffic:**
- 300% increase in organic traffic (85 → 340 monthly visitors)
- 65% improvement in lead quality (commercial vs. residential ratio)
- 45% increase in time on site (better qualified visitors)

**Business Impact:**
- Average project value: $12K → $28K (133% increase)
- 40% increase in commercial project bookings
- First-time wins on 3 municipal bids previously awarded to competitors
- 6-month project backlog (up from 2-3 weeks)

**Revenue Growth:**
- Monthly revenue increase: $48K → $85K
- Year-over-year growth: 180%
- ROI on marketing investment: 890% in first year

### Key Success Factors
1. **Technical Authority:** Detailed specification guides positioned them as experts
2. **Commercial Focus:** Stopped chasing low-value residential leads
3. **Visual Portfolio:** Before/after photos of complex installations
4. **Certification Prominence:** ASBA credentials front and center
5. **Local Dominance:** #1 rankings for commercial court searches in 12 cities

### Client Testimonial
*"Hard Court Marketing completely transformed our business. We went from competing on price to being the premium choice. The quality of our leads improved dramatically - we're now getting calls from facility managers and municipal departments, not homeowners looking for the cheapest option. Our average project value has more than doubled."*
**- Trevor Johnson, Owner, Precision Pro Courts**
